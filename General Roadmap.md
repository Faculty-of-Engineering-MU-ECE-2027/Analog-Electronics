# 🔌 Analog Electronics Roadmap

## 🔬 Solid-State Electronics

> **Solid-State Electronics** is one of the fundamental foundations of modern electronics. Study **crystal structures, energy bands, conductors, insulators, semiconductors, intrinsic and extrinsic semiconductors, doping, electrons and holes, carrier concentration, and charge transport**. A strong understanding of these concepts will help you understand the operation of **PN junctions, diodes, BJTs, MOSFETs, and integrated circuits**.
[You can see this playlist for studying Solid state](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLd9hKAUC3AZuo7is-aN45pmfDwJHOqKAj&si=vwdq9eP1roPQdcjI)

> 💡 **Tip:** Focus on understanding how circuits work rather than memorizing formulas. Build a strong foundation in **voltage, current, resistance, and circuit laws** before moving to transistors, amplifiers, and advanced analog circuits.
>
> 🧪 **Practice is essential:** Try to analyze and simulate the circuits you study using tools such as **LTspice, Multisim, or Proteus** before building them physically.
